# Design decision

**Date:** 7 Nov 2026
**Chosen:** Mockup A, "Peta" — [`a-peta.html`](a-peta.html)
**Decided by:** project owner

## Options shown

| Mockup | Idea | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A — Peta (`a-peta.html`) | Indonesian topographic map sheet: national average as sea level, regencies tinted by how far their price sits above or below it | **Chosen** |
| B — Papan harga (`b-papan.html`) | Market price board: painted header, prices set as number tiles | Not chosen |
| C — Papan penunjuk (`c-penunjuk.html`) | Airport-style yellow wayfinding: monumental change number, sticky sign band | Not chosen |

All three use real data from `data/processed/` (6 Nov 2026) via `sample-data.js`, generated by `make_sample_data.py`.

## Why A

- **Local, not generic.** The map of DIY's five regencies is something only this product can show; it fits PRODUCT.md's "local, not generic" principle and the region comparison (F5) becomes visible at a glance.
- **One honest mechanism.** "National average = sea level" turns the difference-from-national number into a picture without inventing anything: every tint comes from a real price on a stated date.
- **Shows craft to recruiters.** The map doubling as the region selector and the trend chart drawn as an elevation profile demonstrate data presentation, not decoration.

## What the build in `web/` must keep

- Collar band (deep sea blue) with wordmark, data date, and region tabs.
- First viewport: the week's biggest rise as the headline (left), the DIY map with commodity select and legend ramp (right).
- Seven stepped tints, no gradients: three sea blues (cheaper than the DIY average), pale coast (within ±3%), sand, ochre, brown (dearer). Changed in phase 5, see below.
- Rises in red, falls in sea blue, always with an arrow. Red and blue mean change over time only.
- Archivo typeface: condensed caps for map lettering and labels, tabular numerals for prices.
- Trend chart as an elevation profile with labelled contour lines.
- Light and dark themes, 360 px without horizontal scroll, footer with sources and the "not an official source" note.

The full direction contract lives in `.impeccable/surfaces/web-index-html.md`.

## Phase 4 resolutions (8 Nov 2026)

1. **Real regency boundaries.** The page map uses geoBoundaries IDN ADM2 (simplified; source BPS, WFP, OCHA ROAP; licence CC BY 3.0 IGO), converted by `web/scripts/build_map.py` into `web/src/diy-map.json`. Credited in the page footer.
2. **Chart library.** No Chart.js: the elevation profile is hand-drawn SVG (`web/src/chart.ts`), smaller and closer to the direction than a restyled library chart.
3. **Layout change from the mockup.** The region comparison table sits under the answer block, beside the map (the left column was empty on desktop). The trend chart sits beside the commodity list and stays in view while scrolling; on phones it comes before the list.

## Phase 5 resolutions (8 Nov 2026)

1. **Sea level is the DIY average, not national.** Owner's choice after the critique. Against national almost every regency sat "under water", so the map said little. Against the DIY average it shows which regencies are cheaper or dearer than the province. The national comparison stays in the region table (PRD F5), now in rupiah and percent.
2. **Dearer ramp starts at sand.** Green read as "good". The dearer steps are now sand, ochre, brown, a blue-to-brown scale that also holds up for red-green colour blindness.
3. **One meaning per colour.** Red and blue mark change over time only, always with an arrow. Difference from national is set in ink with a sign. The sea name is no longer blue, and regency boundaries are ink lines so the pale coast step reads on white.
